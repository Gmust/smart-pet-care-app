# Smart Pet Care App - Preliminary Market Review

## Status and review protocol

This review was performed on 25 July 2026 for the diploma working draft. It is
a desk review of public Google Play listings, not a usability test or a
technical audit.

The inclusion criteria were:

- an Android application available through a public Google Play listing;
- an English-language listing accessible during the review;
- a product directly concerned with organizing care for one or more pets;
- a listing that described at least pet records, reminders, or contact with
  pet-care providers;
- evidence of recent maintenance or an established installation base.

The comparison criteria were defined before recording the product claims:

- multi-pet organization;
- reminders or schedules;
- pet-health or care records;
- provider integration;
- household or professional sharing/export;
- developer-declared data-safety information;
- an informational or AI assistant, if explicitly advertised.

Four products were selected to cover different product positions: a broad
care-record platform, a health-record and clinic-integration product, a
provider-centered product, and a household logging product. New or
low-adoption products were not selected merely to increase the sample.

## Preliminary interpretation

The listings consistently present organization as the central value
proposition: records, schedules, reminders, and access to pet information.
They differ more strongly in the relationship around that data. VitusVet and
PetDesk emphasize interaction with participating providers, 11pets advertises
veterinary record sharing and a separate business product, and PetnotePlus
emphasizes household collaboration and export.

The reviewed listings did not advertise an informational or AI assistant.
That observation does not establish that the products have no such feature; it
means only that an assistant was not stated in the text reviewed on the
observation date. Smart Pet Care therefore should not claim market novelty
from this four-product desk review. The defensible comparison is that its
declared scope combines pet profiles, reminders, notifications, and a
pet-scoped informational assistant in one student project, subject to explicit
non-diagnostic and privacy boundaries.

The Google Play data-safety statements are supplied by each developer and may
change. They do not prove how an application behaves, whether every flow works,
or whether a product meets a security or privacy standard.

## Final evidence required before submission

- Install the selected applications, where access and terms permit, on a named
  Android device or emulator.
- Record the observed application version, country/region, account state, and
  observation date.
- Capture only the minimum screenshots needed to substantiate the comparison;
  redact personal data and respect store/application terms.
- Distinguish an unobserved feature from an absent feature.
- Recheck all listings near submission because features and data-safety
  declarations can change.
- Obtain supervisor approval for the comparison set and the level of product
  detail included in the thesis.
